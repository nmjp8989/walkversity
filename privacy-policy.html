PrivacyPolicy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Walkvertising is committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the information you provide when visiting our site or contacting us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide when you contact us, such as your name, email address, company name and campaign details.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries and quote requests, provide campaign information, communicate about bookings and improve our services and customer experience.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use or disclosure.

Sharing Your Information

We may share information when necessary to deliver or execute a campaign with operational partners, comply with legal requirements or protect our rights, property or safety.

Cookies and Website Tracking

The website’s inquiry forms open a draft in your email application. Your browser settings allow you to manage cookies and similar technologies.

Your Choices

You can ask us to review, correct or update your personal information or remove you from our communications by writing to info@walkvertising.ca.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our practices, services or legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted here with the revised effective date.

Contact Us

For questions about this policy or how we handle your information, email info@walkvertising.ca.
