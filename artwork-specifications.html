Artwork Specifications

Follow these guidelines to ensure your artwork looks sharp, professional and ready for print.

Display Sizes

Main Ad Panel (Back)
22.75″ W × 55.00″ H
(57.8 cm × 139.7 cm)
Upper Panel (Front)
22.75″ W × 23.50″ H
(57.8 cm × 59.7 cm)

File Setup Requirements

  • Include 20 mm (0.79 in) of image bleed on all sides.
  • Use scanned and raster images at a minimum of 300 dpi at final size.
  • Convert all fonts to outlines.
  • Turn off overprint and trapping.
  • Set colours to CMYK for print.

Accepted File Formats

  • AiAIIllustrator
  • IdINDDInDesign
  • PsPSDPhotoshop
  • PDFPDFPrint ready

Important Notes

  • Supply artwork at the exact display sizes.
  • Keep key text and logos inside the safe margins.
  • Use high-resolution images at 300 dpi or higher.
  • Use CMYK colour mode and prepare all elements for print.
