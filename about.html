Our Story

Walkvertising is a Canadian mobile advertising company that helps brands get noticed in the real world.

We specialize in illuminated walking billboards and street-level brand activations that create awareness, drive foot traffic and deliver measurable engagement at events, retail locations and high-traffic areas.
